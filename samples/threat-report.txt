Harbourline Freight - Threat Intel Note (SAMPLE, fictional)
===========================================================

Summary
-------
During triage we observed a phishing campaign delivering a loader. The messages
came from accounts-payable[at]harbour1ine-freight[.]com and linked to a fake
invoice portal at hxxps://invoice-harbourline[.]com/login.

Network indicators
------------------
The loader beaconed to 203.0.113.45 and 198[.]51[.]100[.]23 over HTTPS, and
resolved cdn-update[.]net before pulling a second stage from
hxxp://185.220.101.9/gate.php. A secondary C2 used the IPv6 address
2001:db8:dead:beef::1337.

Do not block these internal hosts seen in the same logs:
10.10.10.10, 192.168.1.50, 127.0.0.1
